The ritual

Four steps between you and fuel

No blending, no mess. Each pod locks into the base and releases only when you're ready to drink.

Step 1

Select Pod

Choose from Protein, Energy, Electrolytes, Wellness or Focus depending on your day.

Step 2

Attach Pod

Click the glass pod into the base cap — it seats with a firm, satisfying snap.

Step 3

Activate Seal

Twist the collar a quarter turn to break the seal and release the nutrients into water.

Step 4

Mix & Shake

Give it fifteen seconds of shake — the internal mesh disperses everything evenly.

Why glass

Built to be refilled, not thrown away

Every part of Purevita is designed against a single question: what happens to this after use?

Reusable glass pods

Pods are returned, sterilised, and refilled through our exchange program instead of landing in a bin.

Zero single-use plastic

No sachets, no plastic seals — the entire delivery system is glass, silicone, and stainless steel.

Mycelium packaging

Shipping trays are grown from mushroom mycelium, compostable in your garden within 45 days.

Fine print, kept short

Shipping, guarantees & returns

Orders dispatch within 24 hours and arrive in 2–5 business days across India. Every kit ships in compostable mycelium packaging, no plastic fillers.

If your bottle or pod cracks from normal use within 12 months, we replace it free — no receipt required, just your order email.

Unopened Starter Kits can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened pods aren't returnable for hygiene reasons, but we'll help you find the right flavour next time.
